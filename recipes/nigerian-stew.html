Description

This is a stew that originates from Nigerai as the name implies. It is a perfect mix of savory, sweet, and spicy.
It is made by frying the pepper base which brings out an intense flavor.

It's not too watery, nor too thick, it's just right when eating it with your choice carbs(rice or spagetti).

Ingredients:

  • Beef
  • Onions
  • Vegetable or Palm Oil
  • Bouillion cubes (Maggi, Knorr, or any other brand of your choice)
  • Curry powder & thyme
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Habenero(to taste)
