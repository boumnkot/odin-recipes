Description

Egusi Soup is prepared using grinded egusi seeds, mixed with spinach, and Nigerian stew mix(tomatoes, onions, and habeneros aka rodo).
It is fried using a mix of palm oil and any available seed oils.

Egusi soup soup uses stock fish, biscuit bones, and beef/goat meat for its protein. It is a spicy stew. It is seen as a poor man soup in Nigerian cuisine

Ingredients:

  • egusi powder
  • cubbed beef
  • salt to taste
  • palm oil
  • spinach
  • 1 big onion, chopped
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • few portion of bitter leaf
  • Stock fish
